Security & Privacy

🔒 Data Encryption

Your personal information is encrypted and stored locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

🏠 Local Storage

All your data stays on your computer. The extension works completely offline and never transmits your information over the internet.

⏰ Session Timeout

For added security, auto-fill functionality automatically disables after periods of inactivity.
